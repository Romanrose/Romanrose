I am an MSc Software Engineering candidate at [Xiamen University](https://www.xmu.edu.cn/), preparing for work at the intersection of AI research and LLM systems engineering.

My research interests are context-aware AI, agent memory, long-horizon task management, and reliable evaluation for LLM-based systems. I care about how an AI system represents, retrieves, updates, and uses context over time.

I enjoy reading technical papers, turning difficult ideas into clear notes, building small open-source tools, and learning by making things that other people can use.

Currently, I am building [LLM.learn](https://github.com/Romanrose/llm_learn) as a learning and experimentation space for LLM systems, while developing [PRISM](https://github.com/Romanrose/romanrose.github.io) as my personal research and technical portfolio.
